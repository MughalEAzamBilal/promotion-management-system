THE 7-STAGE PROMOTION ENGINE Follow these 7 stages for every DPC cycle

Managing district promotions is streamlined into 7 sequential stages. Follow this operational roadmap to guarantee statutory compliance under SED Service Rules 2014.

1
👥 1. Teachers Directory, Data Quality & Excel Import
Stage: DATA
→ Import, verify, corrections & data integrity

Maintain the district teacher roster. Use Bulk Excel Import to onboard staff, review self-verifications, monitor data quality gaps, and inspect blue (edited) and green (new additions) audit cells.

Teachers Directory Excel Import Data Quality Monitor
2
🏫 2. Vacancies & Quotas
Stage: VACANCY
→ Sanctioned posts + working strength + quota

Record sanctioned posts and working strength for each target promotional cadre (EST, SST, HM). Set statutory promotion quota percentages (e.g. 50% In-Service Quota) to define Zone 1.

Manage Vacancies
3
👴 3. Retirements & Superannuation
Stage: RETIREMENT
→ Age 60+ review & bulk retirement

Audit teachers turning 60 years of age. Bulk-retire them with official retirement order numbers to disqualify them from in-service promotion and vacate positions.

Review Retirements
4
📅 4. DPC Schedule & Agenda
Stage: DPC
→ Create meeting & review cadre-wise retiring counts

Schedule upcoming DPC sessions with start and end dates. The system calculates upcoming retiring counts cadre-wise under each scheduled date. Editable before end date.

DPC Meeting Schedule
5
⚙️ 5. Promotion Scrutiny, Pre-Flight & Official Runs
Stage: SCRUTINY
→ Run eligibility + seniority + quota zones + freeze rolls

Verify the 9-point Pre-Flight checklist, run statutory scrutiny across 8 conditions, organize candidates into Zones 1, 2, and 3, and manage immutable frozen rolls (Draft → Finalized → Locked).

Promotion Scrutiny Roll Promotion Runs Archive
6
📄 6. Official Gazette & PDF Management
Stage: GAZETTE
→ Configure stamp/signatories + generate PDF

Toggle the official circular stamp seal on or off. Add, edit, or remove DPC committee signatories (Chairman, Secretary, Members). Export high-resolution official gazettes.

PDF Hub & Stamps
7
🏛️ 7. DPC Decisions & Seniority Appending
Stage: DECISION
→ Promote / Defer / Supersede → update records

Enter official committee determinations. Promoted candidates automatically receive their new cadre designation, pay scale, and promotional seniority rank (max + 1) while retaining feeder seniority.

Execute Decisions
Mental Model: DATA → VACANCY → RETIREMENT → DPC → SCRUTINY → GAZETTE → DECISION

TEACHER RESPONSIBILITY ROADMAP Your complete promotion journey

As a teacher in the School Education Department, your responsibility follows 6 simple steps to guarantee you are fully eligible when DPC meetings are convened.

1
🏠 1. Dashboard & Attention Notices
CHECK
→ What needs my attention?

Check your Data Completeness Score and read official notifications. Any missing degree fields or pending service entries will be highlighted here.

My Dashboard
2
👤 2. My Profile & Identifiers
COMPLETE
→ Is my personal information correct?

Verify your CNIC, father name, date of birth, contact number, and school station. Your date of birth is crucial for age precedence seniority tie-breaking.

Review Profile
3
🎓 3. Educational & Professional Qualifications
COMPLETE
→ Are all my degrees complete?

Inspect the "Data Audit & Missing Values" column. Fill any missing subjects, marks, or boards. Ensure professional teaching degrees (B.Ed/ADE) are marked "Yes". You can add new degrees at any time!

My Qualifications
4
🏢 4. Service History & Postings
VERIFY
→ Are my appointment, regularization and postings correct?

Verify your regular appointment date—this determines your seniority length. Once satisfied with personal, educational, and service history, click "Verify My Data".

Service History
5
📝 5. Correction Request
CORRECT IF NEEDED
→ I need to change something that is already locked.

After self-verification, records lock to protect data integrity. If a change is needed, tick the box next to the locked cell, write the proposed value, and submit with proof for admin approval.

Correction Portal
6
📋 6. Official Seniority Status
VIEW STATUS
→ Where do I stand officially in my district cadre?

Inspect your statutory seniority rank number, regular service length, and position relative to quota zones (Zone 1 Promotion Zone).

View Seniority Roll
7
📈 7. Promotion Readiness & Statutory Simulator
PROMOTION AUDIT
→ Am I eligible under the 8 statutory conditions?

Audit your promotion readiness across Feeder Cadre, Service Length, Degrees, B.Ed, ACRs, and Disciplinary Checks with clear pass/fail checkmarks, and run the real-time simulator.

My Promotion Readiness Promotion Simulator
Mental Model: CHECK → COMPLETE → VERIFY → CORRECT IF NEEDED → VIEW STATUS
