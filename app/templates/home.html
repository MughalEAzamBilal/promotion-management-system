Statutory System Architecture

Engineered for Merit, Transparency & Speed

Comprehensive digitisation eliminating clerical errors and ensuring non-discretionary compliance with the Department's gazetted rules.

8-Point Statutory Scrutiny

Automated scrutiny of Designation match, Regular Service length, Prescribed Academic Degree, Professional Teaching Degree (B.Ed/M.Ed), Profile Clearance, PEEDA inquiry status, ACR scores, and Vacancy Quota zones.

Seniority-cum-Fitness Roll

Continuous grade service ordering with strict Date of Birth (DOB) tie-breaking precedence. Ensures senior candidates are considered ahead without subjective interference.

Dual Template Bulk Sync

Dedicated Multi-Sheet Excel templates for synchronized import and bulk qualification updates, featuring 80+ pre-coded statutory degrees and university catalogs.

Departmental Password Helpdesk

Secure self-service password reset requests verified against CNIC, Personnel No, Phone, and Email, followed by administrator dispatch via official SMS or Email.

Gazette PDF & DPC Rolls

Instantly render high-resolution Landscape PDF Gazettes and Excel workbooks for Departmental Promotion Committees (DPC) with candidate scrutiny breakdowns.

Inactivity Timeout & Audit

30-minute idle session timeout protection with interactive warning modal and an immutable audit trail capturing all administrative verifications and logins.
