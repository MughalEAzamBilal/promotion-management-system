Official Guide: How to Fill Spreadsheet Templates Correctly

Follow statutory formatting standards to ensure zero validation errors, accurate seniority ranking, and instant promotion eligibility matching.

Multi-Sheet Master (.xlsx) Dedicated Education (.xlsx)
Sheet 1: Teachers_Data (Personal Biodata, Posting & Service Profile)

Contains one row per teacher. Provides core profile details used to establish feeder seniority, cadre scale, and district placement.

Primary Sheet
Column Header Type / Required Valid Format / Values Statutory Purpose & Guidelines
CNIC Mandatory 35201-1234567-9 or 13 digits Master Unique Identifier. Used to establish teacher login accounts and link multiple qualifications in Sheet 2.
Full_Name Mandatory Text (e.g. Muhammad Ahmad) Official name as recorded in departmental service book and CNIC.
Father_Name Mandatory Text (e.g. Abdul Rehman) Father or husband name for official gazette identification.
Gender Mandatory Male or Female Ensures correct allocation in male/female school vacancies and quotas.
Date_of_Birth Mandatory YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 1985-04-12) Used for: (1) Age 60+ Superannuation automatic retirement checks, and (2) Seniority tie-breaking (older candidate ranks senior when regularization dates are identical).
Current_Designation Mandatory PST, EST (Science), EST (General), EST (Arabic), SST (Science), etc. Teacher's present cadre. Used as the Feeder Post when evaluating eligibility for target promotion posts under Service Rules 2014.
Current_BS Mandatory Integer: 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 Basic Pay Scale of the teacher's current post.
Appointment_Date Mandatory YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2010-09-15) First appointment date into government service.
Regularization_Date Mandatory YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2013-10-19) Statutory Seniority Anchor. The promotion engine calculates total regular service length and seniority precedence starting from this date.
District Mandatory e.g. Lahore, Kasur, Rawalpindi Administrative district. DPC promotion rolls and vacancy quotas are filtered strictly by district.
School_Name Mandatory e.g. Govt High School Central Lahore Current place of posting.
Seniority_Number Optional Integer (e.g. 42) Official district seniority list serial number. If omitted, seniority defaults to regularization date order.
Personnel_No Optional 8-digit number (e.g. 31245678) Accountant General / SAP Personnel number for payroll and cross-departmental auditing.
Current_Status Optional Active, Retired, On Leave, Suspended Defaults to Active. Teachers marked Retired are automatically excluded from promotion consideration.
PEEDA_Inquiry Optional None or inquiry details Any active disciplinary inquiry under PEEDA Act 2006. If pending, promotion is automatically placed on hold.

Sheet 2: Qualifications (Multi-Teacher Academic & Professional Degrees)

Allows recording multiple educational degrees (Matric, F.Sc, B.Sc, M.Sc, B.Ed) for any number of teachers in a single unified sheet.

Multi-Row Supported
How Multi-Teacher Qualifications Linking Works:

Every row in the Qualifications sheet must have the teacher's exact CNIC. To add 5 degrees for one teacher, simply write 5 consecutive rows with the same CNIC. The system automatically links each degree to that teacher's profile without duplicating the teacher!

Column Header Type / Required Valid Format / Values Statutory Purpose & Guidelines
CNIC Mandatory 35201-1234567-9 Foreign Key. Must match a valid CNIC in Teachers_Data or an existing teacher in the database.
Degree_Level Mandatory Matric, Intermediate, Bachelor, BS-4Years, Master, M.Phil, PhD, Professional Teaching Standard educational tier. Standardized naming ensures correct seniority and quota calculation.
Degree_Name Mandatory e.g. M.Sc Chemistry, B.Ed (1.5 Years), B.Sc (Botany, Zoology, Chemistry) Official degree title. For fastest automatic validation, copy exact names from the Predefined_Qualifications sheet.
Major_Subject Mandatory e.g. Chemistry, English Literature, Physics, Arabic Crucial for Cadre Matching. Required to qualify for specialized promotion posts like EST (Science), EST (Arabic), or SST (English).
Minor_Subject Optional e.g. Zoology, Botany, Mathematics Secondary or subsidiary subjects.
Passing_Year Mandatory 4-digit year (e.g. 2014) Year of degree completion / issuance of official transcript.
Total_Marks & Marks_Obtained Optional Numeric (e.g. 850 out of 1200) Percentage and division are computed automatically if marks are entered.
Division Optional First, Second, Third, or CGPA Academic merit standing.
Board_University Mandatory e.g. University of the Punjab (PU), Lahore, BISE Lahore Issuing educational authority or HEC-recognized university.
Is_Professional Crucial Yes or No Statutory Professional Teaching Requirement. Must be marked Yes for B.Ed, M.Ed, ADE, B.S.Ed, M.A Education, or DPE. The promotion engine will reject candidates lacking a verified professional teaching degree!

Sheet 3: Predefined_Qualifications (Statutory Catalog per Service Rules 2014)

Built-in reference library containing 80+ statutory degrees and approved subject specializations recognized by SED and HEC.

Statutory Reference
Why use Predefined Degree Titles?

When evaluating candidates for promotion (e.g. PST → EST Science BS-15), the promotion engine checks if the teacher possesses an authorized academic degree in the required subject (e.g. Physics, Chemistry, Biology) and a recognized professional degree (B.Ed/ADE).

Copying exact titles from Sheet 3 guarantees that the evaluation engine awards PASS status on qualification scrutiny instantly.

Recognized Professional Teaching Degrees:
  • B.Ed (1.5 / 2.5 / 4.0 Years)
  • Associate Degree in Education (ADE)
  • B.S.Ed (Bachelor of Science in Education)
  • M.Ed (Master of Education)
  • M.A Education
  • Diploma in Physical Education (DPE) (for Physical Education posts)
  • Shahadat-ul-Almia (recognized for Arabic / Islamic Studies posts)

Best Practices, Formatting Rules & Common Pitfalls

Follow these golden rules to avoid skipped rows, validation warnings, or incorrect seniority ordering.

Validation Checklist
What You MUST Do (Best Practices)
  • Use Standard Date Format: Always use YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2014-03-15). Excel cells formatted as Standard Date or Text both work correctly.
  • Ensure CNIC Consistency: Always double-check that the CNIC in the Qualifications sheet exactly matches the CNIC in Teachers_Data.
  • Fill Regularization Date: The system bases service seniority strictly on regular service. If regularization date is blank, it falls back to appointment date.
  • Mark B.Ed as Professional: Always ensure Is_Professional = Yes for teaching degrees. Leaving it as No will cause the promotion engine to flag the candidate as lacking a professional degree.
  • Review the Preview Summary: Before clicking "Confirm & Import", inspect the validation preview for any warnings or skipped rows.
What to Avoid (Common Pitfalls)
  • Do NOT Rename Column Headers: The import parser looks for exact column names like CNIC, Full_Name, Current_Designation. Modifying them causes column mapping failure.
  • Do NOT Alter Sheet Names: Keep the sheet tab titles as Teachers_Data and Qualifications.
  • Avoid Malformed CNICs: Avoid extra spaces or text inside CNIC cells. Use clean 13 digits (e.g. 35201-1234567-9 or 3520112345679).
  • Do NOT Delete Header Rows: Keep Row 1 as the column header row. Data rows start from Row 2.
  • Don't Worry About Duplicates (Upsert Safe): You don't need to manually filter out teachers who already exist. The system automatically detects existing CNICs and updates their records instead of creating duplicate profiles.
Automated Teacher Portal Credentials: When teachers are newly added through Excel, the system automatically provisions secure portal credentials for them: Username = Teacher's CNIC and Default Password = Teacher@123. Teachers can log in immediately to review their records, complete missing values, and verify their data.
