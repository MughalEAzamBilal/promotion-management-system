Seniority-cum-Fitness & 2:1 Panel Policy

1. Cadre Seniority: Non-selection promotions are evaluated strictly in order of official civil service seniority position.

2. Tie Resolution: If two candidates share the exact same seniority position, precedence is determined by Date of Birth (greater age).

3. Consideration Panel (2:1 Ratio): For promotion up to BS-18, the consideration panel operates on a 2:1 ratio (2 candidates scrutinized per promotional vacancy).
